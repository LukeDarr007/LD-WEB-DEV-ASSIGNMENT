About Us

Discover who we are, what we do, and why Cantor College is a leader in Computing and Design education.

Who We Are

Cantor College was established in 1989 to specialise in Computing and Design education. We are dedicated to giving students the knowledge, skills and industry experience needed to succeed in their future careers.

Our Mission

We aim to deliver high-quality teaching, hands-on learning opportunities, industry engagement, and a supportive environment where every student can thrive.

What Makes Us Unique

  • Computing & Design specialist institution
  • Industry-accredited courses (including BCS)
  • Strong links with employers & researchers
  • Modern facilities designed for innovation
  • Supportive, diverse student community

Our Campus

Our purpose-built 9,500m² building houses specialist facilities including computing laboratories, design studios, makerspaces, collaborative learning areas, a lecture theatre, and student support zones.

Full details are available on the Facilities Page.

Our Community

Students, staff, and researchers from around the world come together to create a collaborative and inspiring environment where technology and creativity thrive.
