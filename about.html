About Cantor College

Cantor College is one of the region’s leading providers of Computing and Design education – a diverse community of students, academic staff, and industry partners. Our mission is simple: to shape futures through innovation, skills, and opportunity.

Founded in 1989, Cantor College has grown into a modern centre for digital creativity and technological innovation. We support students from every background, providing pathways into computing, digital design, software engineering, cybersecurity, and emerging creative industries.

Our teaching is industry-informed, research-led, and focused on real-world impact — helping our students build the skills needed to thrive in rapidly changing global industries.

We are proud to be an ambitious institution committed to excellence, inclusion, regional growth, and preparing graduates who leave Cantor ready to shape the world.
