How to Get Here

By Car

Parking is available on campus. Use Main Street, Sheffield, SC4 2BB for navigation. There are visitor and disabled parking spaces.

By Public Transport

Bus routes 12, 45, and 78 stop within 5 minutes of the campus. The nearest train station is Sheffield Central, 15 minutes by taxi.

By Foot or Bicycle

Bike racks are available on campus. Pedestrian access is from Main Street, with clear signage from local landmarks.
