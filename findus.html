How to Find Us

How to Get Here

Select a transport method below to view directions.

By Car

Parking is available on campus. Use Main Street, Sheffield, SC4 2BB. Visitor and disabled parking spaces are available.

By Public Transport

Bus routes 12, 45 and 78 stop within five minutes of campus. Sheffield Central train station is approximately 15 minutes away by taxi.

By Foot or Bicycle

Bike racks are available on campus. Pedestrian access is via Main Street, with clear signage from nearby landmarks.

Campus Access & Opening Hours
