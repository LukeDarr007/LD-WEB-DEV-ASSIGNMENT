Information for Businesses & Staff

Partner with Cantor College to unlock innovation, expertise, and future-ready talent.

Partner with Cantor College

At Cantor College, we believe industry collaboration drives innovation and growth. Our services support businesses with cutting-edge expertise in Computing, Design, and Technology.

1. Custom Training and Development Programs

We create tailored workforce training in advanced computing, creative design, and emerging technologies. Delivery options include in-person workshops, online modules, and blended learning formats.

2. Research and Development Partnerships

Collaborate with our students and faculty on R&D initiatives. Whether exploring new tech, designing innovative products, or solving technical challenges, our research teams are ready to support your goals.

3. Design and Technology Consultancy

Our experts and top-tier students can assist with software development, UX design, product design, and digital media solutions—bringing creativity and fresh technical insight to your business.

4. Internships and Graduate Recruitment

Access skilled and industry-ready students through our internship and recruitment programmes. We connect businesses with candidates who offer modern skills and practical experience.

5. Event Hosting and Sponsorship

Partner with us for hackathons, design challenges, and technology expos. Sponsorship opportunities offer visibility, networking, and engagement with future digital leaders.

Why Partner With Us?

  • Cutting-Edge Expertise: Our academic programmes reflect the newest trends in technology and design.
  • Innovation at the Core: We cultivate problem-solving and creativity across all departments.
  • Tailored Solutions: Every service is customized to your business needs.
  • Future-Ready Talent: Our students bring modern skills and practical knowledge.
