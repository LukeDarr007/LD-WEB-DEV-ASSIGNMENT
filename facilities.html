Our Facilities

Explore the modern, industry-standard spaces that support learning, creativity, and innovation.

Specialist Computing Labs

Cantor College provides state-of-the-art computer laboratories equipped with high-performance systems for Software Engineering, Networking, Cyber Security, Games Development and Data Science.

Students can access industry tools including virtualised environments, penetration-testing suites, simulation servers, and development platforms.

Design Studios & Creative Workshops

Our dedicated Design spaces include digital studios, 3D workshops, print labs, photography suites, and specialist areas for product, graphic, and interior design.

Students work on real-world projects using professional-grade materials and software.

Why Our Facilities Stand Out

Industry-standard equipment used by professionals

Our facilities are equipped with the same hardware and software used in leading tech, computing, and creative industries, giving students real-world experience in all fields.

Constant updates and modernisation

We regularly upgrade our labs and studios to stay ahead of industry trends, so all our students work with the most up to date and cutting-edge technology.

Specialist technical staff and support

Highly trained technicians will provide hands-on support, maintenance, and expert guidance across all computing and creative spaces.

Secure access and extended opening hours

Our building offers ID card access, CCTV security, and late-night opening hours, perfect for deadlines, projects, and group work.
