Our Facilities

Explore the modern, industry-standard spaces that support learning, creativity, and innovation.

Specialist Computing Labs

Cantor College provides state-of-the-art computer laboratories equipped with high-performance systems for Software Engineering, Networking, Cyber Security, Games Development and Data Science.

Students can access industry tools including virtualised environments, penetration-testing suites, simulation servers, and development platforms.

Design Studios & Creative Workshops

Our dedicated Design spaces include digital studios, 3D workshops, print labs, photography suites, and specialist areas for product, graphic, and interior design.

Students work on real-world projects using professional-grade materials and software.
