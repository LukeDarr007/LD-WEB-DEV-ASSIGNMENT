Computing Courses Information

The College offers undergraduate, foundation year and postgraduate courses in Computing, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, Cyber Security and Forensics, Information Systems and more.

Our courses are British Computer Society accredited and are designed to prepare students for modern industry careers or further postgraduate study such as MSc or PhD pathways. Please find our Computing Courses below:

Computing Courses

  • BSc (Hons) Computer Networks
  • BSc (Hons) Cyber Security with Forensics
  • BSc (Hons) Computer Science
  • BSc (Hons) Computer Science for Games
  • BEng (Hons) Software Engineering
