Library & Resource Centre

Rules Governing the Use of the Library and Resource Centre

Introduction

All students and staff of the College are entitled to register as members of the Library and Resource Centre and to use the facilities for the purpose of their course of study or College employment. Membership may also be extended to other individuals and organisations, usually for a fee. Access for non-members may be arranged; details are available in the External Services leaflet.

Use of facilities implies acceptance of the Library and Resource Centre rules. These rules are designed to protect all users and ensure that the College operates efficiently. Admission is conditional upon observing the rules, which staff are empowered to enforce. Breaches may be subject to disciplinary action.

Rules: Access

  1. Use of Library Facilities: Normally limited to educational and College purposes.
  2. Registration: All individuals must comply with registration procedures and undertake to follow the rules.
  3. Non-members: May be admitted by arrangement for study only. Must sign the Visitors' Book and show identification.
  4. ID Cards: Non-transferable; the cardholder is responsible for items borrowed. Staff may request ID at any time.

Rules: Use of Facilities

  1. Library not responsible for loss or damage of personal belongings. Study tables cannot be reserved with personal items.
  2. All users must conduct themselves quietly; conversation in ‘Silent’ areas is prohibited.
  3. Personal stereos are not allowed.
  4. Mobile phones must be turned off in study areas; use is permitted in stairwells only.
  5. Do not use mains electricity to power personal equipment unless compliant with Health & Safety standards.
  6. Eating and drinking are not permitted in public areas of the Library and Resource Centre.
  7. Misuse of furniture, facilities, or materials may incur charges for damage or loss.
